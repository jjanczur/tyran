Tyran is a task conductor for Claude Code: it interviews you, plans, drives a
team of fresh-context agents through your work — and **refuses to believe any
agent that can't show raw command output as proof**.

<Aside type="note" title="What the badges on this page mean">
Each page below carries the status **that page states about itself**. <Verdict
state="shipped" /> means the mechanism exists and is tested; <Verdict
state="designed" /> means it is a design contract whose epic has not landed.
Nothing here is upgraded in the retelling — if a page says "design contract",
its card says <Verdict state="designed" />.
</Aside>
