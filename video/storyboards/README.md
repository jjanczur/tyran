# Storyboards

One arc, one direction block, per-frame jobs. The shared message, audience,
mood and negative list live in [`../BRIEF.md`](../BRIEF.md) and are not
repeated per concept — a storyboard here says only what is *different* about
its frames.

Frame notation, per the Hyperframes storyboard format:

```
[type]        hook · benefit · proof · cta
[persuasion]  before/after · enumeration · counterexample · callback
[beat]        recognition+tension · aha · resolve+inevitability
[focal]       the one thing the eye lands on
```

`persuasion` and `beat` are never omitted. Without them a frame is "a scene
that shows the tiers"; with them it is "a scene that *proves* the tiers".

---

## The arc, shared by all seven

**Recognition → Mechanism → Proof → Compounding → Landing**

Each concept is a contiguous slice of it. `forgot-28` runs
Recognition→Proof→Landing; `tiers-55` runs Recognition→Mechanism→Landing;
`hero-95` and `onboard-240` run the whole thing.

## The callback, stated explicitly in every concept

A single gold lash-stroke appears early at low weight. In the landing frame
**that same stroke completes and resolves into the jackal lockup**. Stating the
return is what stops a rebuild treating the opening motif as throwaway texture.

- `sting-6` — the whole film *is* the callback, compressed to six seconds.
- `hero-95` — planted at 15.8s as a full-frame crack, paid off at 84.7s as the
  mark's own lash drawing in behind the head.
- `readme-18` — silent, so the mark carries it alone at 15.1s.

## S7 · `sting-6` — 16:9 and 9:16, 6s, no VO

The callback with nothing else attached. Lash draws (0.15–1.2), retracts
(1.15–1.8), mark lands with overshoot (1.45), its own lash follows one beat
behind (1.85), wordmark (2.0), rule (2.32), sub-line (2.5), ambient hold to 6.

**Follow-through is the whole craft here:** the mark's lash resolves *after*
the head, not with it.
